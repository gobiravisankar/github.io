About Me

I'm Gobi Ravisankar, a freelance web developer and QA professional focused on building modern, responsive and user-friendly websites.

I help small businesses, startups, entrepreneurs, personal brands, schools, e-commerce businesses and professionals build a modern web presence — whether that means a brand-new site or improvements to an existing one.

My background spans front-end development, responsive web design, UI implementation, and website testing, including cross-browser testing, QA and API testing. That combination means the sites I build aren't just designed well — they're checked thoroughly before they reach your users.
